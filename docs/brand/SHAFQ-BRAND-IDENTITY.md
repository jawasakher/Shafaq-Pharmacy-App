# SHAFQ (شَفَق) — Brand Identity & Design Rationale

## Overview
SHAFQ (شَفَق) is an Arabic-first healthcare and pharmacy delivery platform designed to connect patients, pharmacists, and delivery drivers with professional precision, compassion, and reliability.

## Brand Concept
The SHAFQ visual identity combines:
1. **Orbital & Circular Continuity**: Representing connected healthcare ecosystems and ongoing patient care.
2. **Medical Dawn Motif**: Conveying hope, renewal, and accessible medical services (matching the Arabic name *Shafaq*, meaning twilight/dawn glow).
3. **Arabic Wordmark**: The official Arabic wordmark **شَفَق** meticulously rendered to preserve Arabic typographic integrity and cultural resonance.

## Core Values
- **Trust & Safety**: Professional healthcare standards, secure consultations, and prescription verification.
- **Accessibility**: Light, welcoming interface design with high contrast and intuitive RTL navigation.
- **Precision**: Clean geometric craftsmanship across all digital touchpoints.
